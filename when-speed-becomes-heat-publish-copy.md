Speed has a hidden enemy. Heat.

Concorde, the SR-71 Blackbird and the X-15 faced the same problem at very different speeds: aerodynamic heating.

Concorde's skin heated up and expanded. The Blackbird used titanium and attachments that accommodated expansion. The X-15 used heat-resistant Inconel X; its record-setting X-15A-2 configuration also carried an ablative coating covered with white sealant.

Three aircraft. Different solutions. Flying faster means engineering for the heat.
