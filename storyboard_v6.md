# Abhimanyu v6: Redesign (storyboard before any generation)

## Why v5 failed
- Abhimanyu was shown full-body and acting. AI can't keep a body consistent across shots: build, posture and gait changed (skinny, limping, hunched).
- Shots illustrated words loosely instead of each one carrying a single clear idea.
- No visual through-line: twenty unrelated pretty pictures.

## What FZ1073 ShortA does (from the Higgsfield history)
1. **Faceless.** The pilot is a white sleeve with four gold stripes, a hand on the floor, a back. Identity comes from one detail.
2. **One object carries the story:** the cockpit door and its red lock light. Closed means trapped; open means saved.
3. **Master sets.** A few master images are made first; every other shot is "same composition as the reference" with one change. The space never drifts.
4. **Low, intimate camera:** floor level, macro handles, sleeves, hands.
5. **Painterly documentary realism,** teal shadows, one saturated colour.

## v6 visual rules
- **Abhimanyu never shows his face and never walks on screen.** He is: an indigo sash with a frayed gold border, a bronze armband, dark curls seen from behind, his hands. That is his "four-stripe sleeve".
- **The story object is the opening:** a narrow gap of light in a wall of bronze shields. It is open in Shot 1, he goes through it in Shot 11, it slams shut in Shot 12, and it is sealed in the last shot.
- **One saturated colour:** the indigo sash (him, alone). Everything else is dust ochre, grey bronze and smoke; the light through the opening is pale gold.
- **2.5D motion:** the camera moves (push, drift, parallax) through still, painterly frames. Motion inside the frame is only dust, smoke, cloth, banners, light, and objects (shields, wheels, a bow). No person walks, runs or fights on camera, so there is nothing to limp.
- **Master sets (made first, everything else derives from them):**
  - M1 **The Opening:** ground-level view of the shield wall with a narrow gap of light.
  - M2 **The Spiral:** high aerial of the Chakravyuha as a labyrinth of shields and spears.
  - M3 **The Sand:** a patch of dusty ground, low angle, for the hand-drawn spiral.
  - M4 **The Warrior:** his back from waist up, with the indigo sash, bronze armband and curls (the identity sheet).
  - M5 **The Wheel:** a heavy wooden chariot wheel, worn bronze rim, in dust.

## Script v6 (Teja's version, minimal edits marked)
> He knew how to get in.
> He didn't know how to get out.
> So why did the Pandavas send Abhimanyu into the Chakravyuha?
> Because on that day... Arjuna was away.
> And Drona had formed a battle array the Pandavas **couldn't** break.  *(was "struggled to break")*
> Abhimanyu knew how to breach it.
> But he admitted... he didn't know how to escape.
> Still, Yudhishthira told him to break the formation...
> and promised the others would follow.
> Abhimanyu charged.
> He broke through.
> Then the opening closed.
> Jayadratha stopped the Pandavas from following.
> Abhimanyu was alone.
> His bow was destroyed.
> His chariot was wrecked.
> His sword was gone.
> So he picked up a chariot wheel...
> and kept fighting.
> But that was the tragedy.
> He was never supposed to be alone.

Only one wording change ("couldn't" is punchier and matches the source: the Pandavas did not know how to break it). "Cakravyuha" is spelled "Chakravyuha" so the voice reads it right.

2. Generate each shot from its master ("same composition as the reference, change only ...").
3. Motion: Wan 2.2 I2V with camera-and-atmosphere-only prompts (no walking/fighting), plus a depth-based parallax pass for the most static frames.
4. Narration: Teja Clone, script v6, calm documentary delivery.
