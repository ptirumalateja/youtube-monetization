## Facts checked
- P(22) = 47.6%, P(23) = 50.7%, P(70) = 99.9% (99.916%), from P(n) = 1 - prod_{k=0}^{n-1} (365-k)/365.
- Pairs: 23 people = 253 (23x22/2); 70 people = 2,415.
- Assumes 365 equally likely days and ignores 29 February; real birthdays are slightly uneven, which only raises the odds.
