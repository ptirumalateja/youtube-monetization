**Description:**
What would really happen if gravity switched off? Not a movie: you wouldn't shoot into space. Everything unattached keeps moving in a straight line while the curved, spinning Earth drops away beneath it. In Hyderabad that takes about 8 seconds to lift you one metre, and about 62 seconds to reach 60 metres.

Then gravity comes back. A 60 m fall takes just 3.7 seconds and you hit at about 124 km/h.

Would you rather float for 10 seconds or fall for 3? Tell me below.
