## Concept
Most diagrams draw the Moon's path around the Sun as a ring of loops. Shrink that drawing to real scale and the loops unwind: the Moon just rides along Earth's orbit, weaving around it. The Sun pulls the Moon harder than Earth does, so its path always curves toward the Sun. It never loops and never goes backwards.

## Facts and sources
- Earth-Sun mean distance 149.6 million km (1 AU); Earth-Moon mean distance 384,400 km. Ratio 389. [NASA Moon fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html), [NASA Earth fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html).
- Sidereal month 27.3217 d; synodic month 29.5306 d; year 365.256 d. 365.256 / 29.5306 = 12.37 lunar months (new-Moon to new-Moon) per year. The ride shows 12 whole lunar months (354 days), so the Moon is back at the same phase when the reel loops. Source: NASA Moon fact sheet.
- Sun's pull on the Moon vs Earth's: GM_sun / (1 AU)² = 5.93 mm/s²; GM_earth / (384,400 km)² = 2.70 mm/s²; ratio 2.20. Computed from the fact-sheet GM values.
- The Moon's path around the Sun is convex (always curves toward the Sun, never loops): [Wikipedia, Orbit of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_of_the_Moon), [Wolfram demonstration, convexity of the lunar orbit about the Sun](https://www.wolframcloud.com/objects/demonstrations/EpicyclesRevisitedConvexityOfLunarOrbitAboutTheSun-source.nb), [Discover, "The Moon that went up a Hill but came down a planet"](https://www.discovermagazine.com/the-moon-that-went-up-a-hill-but-came-down-a-planet-20889). Checked numerically in `window.convexCheck()`: the minimum curvature of the true path is 0.58 of Earth's orbital curvature (positive everywhere).
- The Moon's speed relative to the Sun stays between about 28.8 and 30.8 km/s (Earth 29.8 ± Moon 1.0), so it never moves backwards.
- Simplifications (said in the copy, not on screen): circular orbits in one plane (the real lunar orbit is tilted 5.1° and slightly elliptical; neither changes the convexity). Body sizes are enlarged; distances and the path are true.

## Imagery and sound
- Earth day, night-lights and cloud maps: NASA Blue Marble / Black Marble imagery (NASA Earth Observatory, public domain), taken from the MIT-licensed `three-globe` npm package's example images (the NASA sites are blocked from the build container). Credit line for the description: "Earth imagery: NASA Earth Observatory (Blue Marble)."
- Moon textures: procedural, generated by `gen_moon.py` (ours). Sun, sky and Milky Way: procedural shaders and points (ours).
- Sound: fully synthesised (`sound.mjs`, no credits). Family "orbital glass": a spirograph warble at the loop rate while the usual drawing is on screen, which collapses into one pure tone as the loops unwind; dive whooshes; a low pad with one glass ping per completed lunar month climbing a minor scale; a throbbing gold drone under the pull beams; a crane swell, held breath and a glass-bowl hit on "IT NEVER GOES BACKWARDS"; a rising tone back into frame 0.
