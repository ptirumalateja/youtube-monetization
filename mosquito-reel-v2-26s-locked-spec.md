## Locked visual / UI design system

- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, **30 fps**; readable on daylight phone display.
- Unified **cinematic 2D Ink & Atmosphere 2.0**: silhouettes, botanical/scientific line art, layered parallax, restrained grain and glow.
- Warm amber on nature, teal and deep navy on Earth/science/malaria; avoid uninterrupted orange; light red only for meaningful data markers.
- Cream monospaced text with dark stroke; one headline/fact at a time; avoid top and bottom platform UI zones.
- Geometry, spatial continuity and legible movement beat ornamental effects. No uneven aura movement, no random rings, no exposure pumping, no flash overkill, no unnecessary UI.
- Never let the hero's display scale imply a different fall velocity from similar-depth insects.
- Make transitions causally related: **pond ripple → Earth limb**, **transmission line → Arctic horizon**, complementary scientific/nature shapes thereafter.
- Grade first, fine grain last; sound and video sync QA against final audio.
- Final must be checked at 2 fps contact sheet, 1× phone scale, section joins, boom timing, safe areas, color grade, factual labeling and clean return loop.
