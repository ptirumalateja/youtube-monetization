## Scientific facts / MUST FIX before final upload

- The deepest measured part of the **Mariana Trench** is **Challenger Deep**, approximately **10,935 metres (35,876 ft)** below sea level according to NOAA. Other infographics cite slightly different values; quote an approximate figure rather than an unjustified exact 10,994 m.
- **Mount Everest** is about **8,849 m high above sea level**. A hypothetical Everest standing on the bottom of Challenger Deep would leave approximately **2,086 m** of water above its peak (10,935 - 8,849). This is a hypothetical comparison, not the actual location of Everest.
- **Burj Khalifa:** about **828 m** architectural height; use ~828 m, not 830 m if precision matters.
- **RMS Titanic:** wreck approximately **3,800 m underwater** in the **North Atlantic**, NOT in the Mariana Trench. It is a benchmark for depth only, not a depiction of the wreck's real location.
- **Cuvier's beaked whale:** recorded dives to depths approaching **3,000 m**; treat any **2,992 m** label as a reported extreme, not a routine diving depth.
- A **commercial aircraft at 10,684 m** is an altitude **ABOVE sea level**, never an underwater depth. Better to show its marker above the surface or remove it.
- **Matterhorn height:** approximately **4,478 m above sea level**; it's a height comparison, not an ocean-depth observation.
- Depth axis must run **0 m at ocean surface** and **10,935 m near seabed**, **increasing downward**. The generated poster currently places depth numbers in an inconsistent sequence; correct it before publishing.
- The comparison diagram is **not to scale** and the ocean landscape/objects are juxtaposed for education, not physically colocated.
