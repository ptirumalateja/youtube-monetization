## What happens on screen (V2)

- **0-4 s, AT 8:** one table under the warm lamp. Mom (purple saree, bun) and Dad sit across, the boy on our side with his back to us, three plates with warm food. Mom serves him. Text: "AT 8, YOU ATE TOGETHER EVERY DAY." Calendar tag: 2005 | PARENTS 35.
- **4-9 s, AT 18:** the camera dollies back. He grows up in his chair, takes his bag, shoves the chair back and walks away into the dark toward his desk and bed. Mom lifts the red phone and he picks up while he walks ("MOM CALLING... DAILY"). Text: "AT 18, YOU LEFT." The calendar flips to 2015 | 45 at 7 s.
- **9-14.5 s, AT 25:** one fixed wide shot of the whole distance: the parents at the table in front, him small at his laptop in the back. Mom's hair is grey and Dad is bent, with a stick. Their plates are full, his is empty and grey. She calls and his phone buzzes on the desk, but he doesn't pick up ("MISSED CALL - MOM"). The lamp cools and dims. Text: "AT 25, YOU CALL ONCE A WEEK." with "THEY STILL WAIT EVERY DAY." in gold. Calendar: 2022 | 52.
- **14.5-19 s:** 0.2 s of black, then a slow push-in on his empty chair. Their two cardigans hang over their chairs and the red phone rings by his empty plate. "THEY WAITED 20 YEARS FOR YOU TO GROW UP." then "CALL NOW." Silence from 16 s.

## Facts and sources

The reel is a parable. Its numbers belong to the story: you at 8, 18 and 25; your parents at 35, 45 and 52 (the same 27-year gap all the way, years 2005, 2015 and 2022), "once a week" and "20 years". None of them is presented as data.
