## What happens on screen

- **0-3 s (AGE 8):** Mom, in a saree with her hair in a bun, hands her son his steel tiffin at the family table under the gold lamp. Dad reads the paper behind them. The red phone sits on the table.
- **3-7 s (8 → 18):** he hugs her and grows up inside the hug until he is taller than her. Dad stands to see him off, and he walks into the dark with a backpack.
- **7-13 s (18 → 28):** hard cut to a wide shot. Far away, a cooler lamp clicks on over his desk and bed. Each call is a gold light crossing the dark from the red phone to his phone. He picks up at 18 (DAILY), turns the phone face down at 23 (LATER), doesn't look at 25 (BUSY), and at 28 makes one short call himself (ONCE A WEEK). Over the same years Mom's hair goes grey, Dad stoops and gets a stick.
- **13-17 s:** the camera pushes in on the two of them waiting by the silent phone, with their plates covered. His chair is pushed in on our side of the table in front of a covered plate.
- **17-19 s:** the lamp narrows onto the red phone. It rings once, Mom's hand goes to it, "CALL NOW." appears, and then there is silence. The parents grow old but stay alive.

## Facts and sources

The reel is a parable. Its numbers belong to the story: you go from 8 to 28 and your parents from 38 to 58 (the same 30-year gap the whole way), "10 times a day" and "once a week". None of them is presented as data.
