## Pinned comment
Quick fact check: a 0.1 mm sheet folded n times is 0.1 mm × 2^n thick. 42 folds passes the Moon, 51 the Sun, 103 about 107 billion light-years (the observable universe is about 93 billion). The Guinness record is 12 folds (Britney Gallivan, 2002, 1,219 m of tissue paper); you can't really fold it that far.
