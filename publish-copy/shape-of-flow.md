## Facts and sources

- Magnet and Earth fields are drawn as ideal dipole field lines (real Earth's field is squashed on the sun side and stretched on the night side).
- The flow in the smoke ring and the magnetic field lines of a loop are both circles around the tube; they are the same geometry, not the same physics.
- Tokamak is a simple circular torus with 16 coils, not a real D-shaped cross-section.
