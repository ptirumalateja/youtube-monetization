## On screen
One caption for the whole reel, unchanged from frame 0 to the end (Teja, 3 Nov): **Let their words become / the bricks of your success** (sentence case, Inter, centred above the scene).

V4 (4 Nov 2026): frame 0 is a close-up. The first brick, WEAK, is already flying straight at his face. He slips his head a little to one side, it passes just over him, and it smashes on the floor behind him. Cut wide: four slate men stand well out in front of a clean 16 x 6 wall and throw YOU CAN'T, GIVE UP, LOSER, NOT ENOUGH, YOU'LL FAIL and QUIT. He catches every one in front of his face and sets it into the last 6 gaps of the top row. Each catch has its own thunk, rising in pitch. Close-up: the big red YOU'LL NEVER MAKE IT slams into the finished wall in front of him and bounces back at their feet. Wide: he hops up onto his own wall and stands tall, and the men lower their heads. The last 0.4 s dissolves back into frame 0.
