## Sound
Synthesised in `tools/cant-inspire-change/sound.py`: a rising glass-chime note each time a glass lights up, a pour shimmer on each stream, a lid slam with a sour bell, two knocks, a lid-strain squeak, and a warm pad that swells into the ending. No music; the `_silent` file is for adding a trending song in-app.
