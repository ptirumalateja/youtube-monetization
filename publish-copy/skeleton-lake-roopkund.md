## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Why Were Greek Skeletons Found in a Himalayan Lake? #shorts
**Alt title:** Roopkund: The Lake Where the Dead Are 1,000 Years Apart
**Description:** Every summer, the ice on Roopkund Lake (5,000 m, Uttarakhand) melts and gives back hundreds of skeletons. A 2019 ancient DNA study found two main groups who died roughly 1,000 years apart, around 800 AD and around 1800 AD. The second group's closest genetic match is present-day Greece and Crete.
Source: Harney et al. 2019, Nature Communications 10, 3670.
