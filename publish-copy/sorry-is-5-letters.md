# Sorry Is 5 Letters (v2, 13.6 s, 9:16)

Stickman reel in the Silence Is Distance look: two people stand back to back after a fight. The word SORRY hangs over them as five gold blocks that get heavier every day (DAY 1 -> 30) until they crush them. He finally types it, the giant letters shrink into one small message, the weight is gone and they turn around and hold hands.
