## Fact notes
- Col. B. Santosh Babu, born 13 Feb 1983, Suryapet; Sainik School Korukonda; CO 16 Bihar from Dec 2019.
- Galwan clash 15 June 2020; 20 Indian soldiers killed; no firearms used (border agreements).
- Maha Vir Chakra (posthumous), announced Republic Day 2021, presented 23 Nov 2021. Second-highest wartime gallantry award after the Param Vir Chakra.
