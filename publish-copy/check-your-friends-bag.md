## Facts and sources

The reel is a parable and makes no factual claim on screen. The rock labels (NO ONE CARES, ALONE, TOO LATE, TIRED, EVERYONE AHEAD, NOT GOOD ENOUGH, WHAT'S THE POINT, I CAN'T) are common self-talk, not statistics. The idea that a friend asking and helping can matter is consistent with suicide-prevention guidance that asking someone directly how they are does not increase risk (e.g. Dazzi et al. 2014, *Psychological Medicine* 44(16)); keep the copy general and do not present the reel as clinical advice. If comments raise self-harm, reply with a local helpline (YouTube shows crisis panels automatically for related searches).
