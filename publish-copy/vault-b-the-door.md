## Facts
- Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple is in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. There are six main vaults, named A to F.
- In June to July 2011, vaults A, C, D, E and F were opened and inventoried. The widely reported value is over ₹1 lakh crore (about US$22 billion at the time). Reported items include gold idols, a gold chain about 18 feet long, sacks of gold coins, crowns and precious stones.
- Vault B is still unopened. Its door has no visible bolt or keyhole and carries two cobra images.
- Naga Bandham (or Naga Pasam): the belief that the door is sealed by a snake-binding chant, and that only a perfect Garuda mantra recital can open it. Treat this as legend.
- T. P. Sundararajan was the former IPS officer whose petition led to the vaults being opened. He died in July 2011, weeks after the inventory began. Believers linked his death to the curse. Present it as "believers say".
