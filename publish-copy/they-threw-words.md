## Facts and sources

The reel is a parable and makes no factual claim. The idea that the bricks others throw can become your foundation is a common motivational saying, often credited to the US newscaster David Brinkley ("A successful man is one who can lay a firm foundation with the bricks others have thrown at him"). We could not find a primary source for that wording, so the copy doesn't quote or credit him. If a commenter asks, say "often attributed to David Brinkley".
