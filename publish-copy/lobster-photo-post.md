## Facebook caption
A lobster is soft inside a hard shell that never grows. 🦞

So when the shell gets too tight, it doesn't push through the pain. It hides under a rock, cracks the old shell open, and climbs out. For a few weeks it's soft and vulnerable while a bigger shell hardens. Then it does it again, dozens of times in its life.

If it never felt the squeeze, it would never grow.

Rabbi Abraham Twerski put it best: the stress is the signal. Times of discomfort are times when we can grow.

What shell are you outgrowing right now? 👇
