## Facts and sources

The reel is a parable, not a factual claim. It retells a widely shared story, often called "push the rock" or "the man and the rock", whose author is unknown. In it, a man is told to push a huge rock. He pushes it for months and it never moves. In the end he is told that the task was to push, not to move the rock, and that his strength had grown all along. Don't attribute it to a named author. The 365 days, the mockers and the lift are our own staging.
