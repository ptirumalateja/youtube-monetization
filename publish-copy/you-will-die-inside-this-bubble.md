## Facts and sources
- Humans ever born, about 117 billion: Population Reference Bureau estimate (2022).
- Sun's speed around the galaxy, about 230 km/s ≈ 830,000 km/h, plus Sagittarius A* at the centre: standard astronomy values.
- Voyager 1: launched 5 Sep 1977; crossed the heliopause 25 Aug 2012 at about 121.6 AU (NASA/JPL). Voyager 2: crossed 5 Nov 2018 at about 119 AU.
- 1 light-day ≈ 25.9 billion km. Voyager 1 reaches it in mid-November 2026 (NASA/JPL distance tracker). Its one-way light time is about 23.5 h in early November 2026.
- Cosmic rays come mostly from supernova remnants, i.e. exploding stars. The heliosphere deflects most of the galactic cosmic rays that would otherwise reach the inner solar system.
