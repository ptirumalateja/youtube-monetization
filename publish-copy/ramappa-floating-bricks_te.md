Facts used: Ramappa (Rudreswara) temple, Palampet, Mulugu district, Telangana. Built 1213 CE in the Kakatiya era (Recherla Rudra, general of Ganapati Deva). The gopuram/vimana uses light porous bricks that float on water. The foundation uses the sandbox technique (pit filled with sand) for earthquake resistance. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2021.
