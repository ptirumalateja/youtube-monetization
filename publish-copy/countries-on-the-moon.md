Fixes to the VidIQ plan:
- **Nepal is not bigger than Bangladesh.** Bangladesh is 148,460 km² and Nepal 147,181 km² (national figures 147,570 and 147,516). They are the same size to within 1%. Population: Bangladesh about 173 million, Nepal about 30 million, so about 6×, not 11×. The reel says "SAME SIZE AS BANGLADESH. WITH 1/6 OF THE PEOPLE."
- **Pakistan uses 796,095 km² (47.6), not 881,913 (43).** The larger figure includes Gilgit-Baltistan and PoK, which are already inside India's 3,287,263 km² and India's outline in this video.

Outlines: Natural Earth 10m admin-0, India point-of-view edition, each scaled by under 4% so its area matches the official figure (coastline and water differences).
