## Facebook caption
Most people grab the ₹1 crore. For 23 days, they're right. 👀

Here's why the doubled rupee feels so useless and then suddenly isn't: every new day adds more than all the previous days put together. Day 10 is only ₹1,023. Day 20 is still about ₹10 lakh. But day 24 alone adds ₹83 lakh, and the last day adds more than ₹53 crore, half of the whole ₹107 crore in a single day.

That's exponential growth. Your brain guesses in straight lines, so the slow start fools almost everyone. It's the same reason a savings habit, a skill or a small business looks pointless for months and then takes off. The boring days aren't wasted. They're the base the big days stand on.
