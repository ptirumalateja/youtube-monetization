**Description**
In a dream you fall through impossible worlds and never once ask "is this real?" Here is why.

During REM sleep, the brain regions that help with logic and reality-checking (the prefrontal cortex) become less active, while emotion circuits like the amygdala stay highly active. Your body is also temporarily paralysed (REM atonia) while your mind runs a full simulation, stitched together from your memories, recent events and random ideas, in real time.

So the fear, the joy and the loss feel completely real, even though the world isn't.
