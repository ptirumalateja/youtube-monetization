## Facts
- How long energy takes to random-walk from the Sun's core to its surface is model-dependent: published estimates run from about 10,000 to about 170,000 years, and 100,000 is a common round figure. The caption gives the range.
- In reality, light inside the Sun is absorbed and re-emitted countless times, so "one photon" is a storytelling device. Between scatterings a real photon travels in straight lines; the curved trails are stylised. The `THOUGHT EXPERIMENT · NOT TO SCALE` footer is on every frame.
- Sun to Earth averages 8 min 20 s (about 499 s) at 299,792 km/s.
- Earth reflected in the pupil is a poetic reflection; a real eye looking at the sunset would reflect the Sun.
- "Older than civilization": the first cities are about 6,000 years old and farming about 12,000.
