## Facts checked
| Claim | Check |
|---|---|
| Jupiter has 318x Earth's mass | NASA fact sheet: 1.898e27 kg / 5.97e24 kg = 318 |
| Jupiter "shields" Earth | Debated. Simulations (e.g. Horner and Jones, 2008 onward) find Jupiter ejects many long-period comets but also perturbs some asteroids and comets inward. Captions say so instead of claiming full protection. |
| Shoemaker-Levy 9, 1994, 21 pieces, Earth-size scars | NASA: 21 fragments hit 16-22 July 1994; the largest (G) left a dark spot about Earth-size |
| 2009 impact scar the size of the Pacific Ocean | NASA/JPL, July 2009 (Anthony Wesley's discovery), scar about Pacific-sized |
| Sun 109x Earth, 1.3 million Earths by volume | 1,392,700 km / 12,742 km = 109; 109^3 = 1.3 million |
| Scale ratios 6,500x, 100 million x, 930,000x | Same table as The Scale Ladder reel (`scale-ladder-publish-copy.md`) |
| Sunlight takes 8 minutes; light crosses the Milky Way in ~100,000 years | 1 AU / c = 8.3 min; ~100,000 light-years across |
| Sorry caption | Opinion and advice, no statistics claimed |
