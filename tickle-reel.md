# Why can't you tickle yourself? — FROZEN v3 (15 Oct 2026)

Format B reel, 23.2 s, 4:5 scene window (1080x1350, 30 fps), voiceover + synthesised sound. **Frozen: no redo's.** Owner sign-off: "solid AF, no changes, freeze it". Supersedes the early plan (flat-vector "style B", 32 s) and the Wan-based openings.

## Final look (what "Format B" means now)
Teal contour-line art on near-black, one 3D world, one continuous camera. Bone white = the real hand/movement, teal = prediction, red = contact/surprise only. Hand, palm, brain and macro art were generated by the owner in Meta AI (same prompt family: thin parallel teal lines on dark navy) and live as alpha PNGs in `tools/tickle/three/assets/`; they are animated as layered sprites in three.js.

## The grammar that made it work (reuse for any Format B reel)
1. **Time-offset ghost:** the ghost is the same hand shifted ahead in time (0.6 s) along the same path. It must land exactly on the contact point as part of its own approach and then stay perfectly still; never slide or re-orient it at the merge.
2. **Heading is held when the path ends** (computing direction from a stopped path gives atan2(0,0)=0 and the hand snaps flat).
3. **Depth stack:** background contour field that bends toward the target, glass layers behind the hand, fine foreground lines, dust; the camera orbits with parallax. No planes crossing the hand, no vertex warp on fingers.
4. **Continuity by match-moves:** macro push on contact, a line that leaves the contact and carries the camera into the brain, a return line to the SAME palm, then the surprise strikes there.
5. **The surprise is an absence:** the other hand arrives on an unpredicted path with no ghost; contours tear into red; one burst, shake, 12% flash.
6. Hold nothing static for more than ~1 s; one object per beat.
