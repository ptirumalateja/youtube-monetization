**Description**
He knew how to get in. He didn't know how to get out.

On the thirteenth day of the Kurukshetra war, Arjuna was drawn away and Drona formed the Chakravyuha. Abhimanyu told the Pandavas he could break in but did not know the way out. Yudhishthira asked him to open the formation and promised the others would follow. Then Jayadratha held the gap, and Abhimanyu was left alone inside.

The real tragedy was never that he didn't know the way out. It was that no one could get in.

Retold from the Drona Parva of the Mahabharata (BORI Critical Edition). The popular story of learning the Chakravyuha in the womb comes from later retellings.
