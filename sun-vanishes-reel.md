## Fact guardrails
- Sun-to-Earth light travel time: about 8 min 20 s (499 s average, orbit varies 8:10 to 8:27).
- In general relativity, changes in gravity propagate at the speed of light. Observation: GW170817 matched light to within ~1 part in 10^15. So Earth would keep orbiting for the same 8 min 20 s.
- Then no sunlight and no gravity: Earth leaves its orbit in a straight line at ~30 km/s. Say "straight line", never "shoots into space".
- The Sun cannot actually vanish. Frame as a thought experiment ("What if").
- Do NOT claim exact freeze times. Safe line: "temperatures would fall quickly" (caption only, hedged).
