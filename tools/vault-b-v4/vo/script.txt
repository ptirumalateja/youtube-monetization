They were warned... never to open the door.

Beneath Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Kerala, lie six secret vaults.

In 2011, they opened five.

Gold idols. An eighteen-foot gold chain. Sacks of ancient coins. Crowns. Diamonds.

Over one lakh crore rupees.

Then... they reached Vault B.

No handle. No lock. No keyhole. Just two cobras, guarding the door.

Legend says it is sealed by a Naga Bandham, and only a perfect chant can open it.

Weeks later, the man who fought to open the vaults... was dead.

Vault B is still shut. Would you open it?
