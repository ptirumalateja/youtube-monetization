# Their Words Became His Wall (Slow Light Dark), V2 of They Threw Words

10 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, wide shot. This is the side-on wall version Teja asked for: the stairs V1 (`../words-to-stairs/`) was too close to One Step a Day. Copy: `publish-copy/their-words-became-his-wall.md`.
The pipeline is the same as `../words-to-stairs/README.md`: npm install, copy the two JetBrains Mono TTFs into `fonts/`, `render.cjs meta|frames|stills`, `mix.py OUT.wav`, `compose.py RENDER OUT`.

- `scene.js`: a wide shot like the reference. Three small slate men stand on the left. The wall is 10 x 5 blocks in running bond, word faces toward camera, and 44 of its blocks are already standing on frame 0 (their older words, dimly lit). He stands behind it on the right, catches the last 6 blocks and sets them word-out into the gaps of the top row. The red block (held low by the third man from frame 0) smashes into the wall's left end, which shakes along with the camera, and bounces back to land at the men's feet. He rests his hands on top of the wall.
- `mix.py`: no voices (Teja: just brick-throwing sounds). A wind-up swish and release footfall, a recorded whoosh per throw (Tomlija 175183, CC BY 3.0), catch slaps, stone placements with ember crackle, shuffle steps, a held-breath duck, then the slam into the wall and the drop. No music.
