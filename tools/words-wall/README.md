# Their Words Became His Wall (Slow Light Dark), V3 of They Threw Words

10 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, wide shot. This is the side-on wall version Teja asked for: the stairs V1 (`../words-to-stairs/`) was too close to One Step a Day. Copy: `publish-copy/their-words-became-his-wall.md`.
The pipeline is the same as `../words-to-stairs/README.md`: npm install, copy the two JetBrains Mono TTFs into `fonts/`, `render.cjs meta|frames|stills`, `mix.py OUT.wav`, `compose.py RENDER OUT`.

- `scene.js` (V3, rebuilt from `concepts/wall-reel-frame-read.md`): a side-angled wide shot from the front-right, like the reference. Four slate men stand out in front of the wall, 1.5-2 m clear of it (Teja's arrows, 3 Nov). The wall is 16 x 6 plain bricks in running bond (one InstancedMesh, clean, no words), with the last 6 top-row bricks missing on frame 0. He stands behind the wall's right end, facing them. Each brick is thrown on a ballistic arc at his face, caught in front of it with two-hand IK, and set into the next gap; its word floats above it in flight and fades on the catch. The big red brick (0.46 x 0.32, wider than his head) is held low by the front man from frame 0 and thrown at his face, but it slams into the finished wall in front of him and bounces back to land at their feet; he rests his hands on the wall, safe. Lighting: a flat grey backdrop that matches the fog (no horizon line), hemisphere fill, a shadow-casting key from the front-right, a cool rim, and the series lamp over the wall's end. `?cam=x,y,z,tx,ty,tz&fov=` overrides the camera for framing tests.
- `mix.py`: no voices (Teja: just brick-throwing sounds). A wind-up swish and release footfall, a recorded whoosh per throw (Tomlija 175183, CC BY 3.0), catch slaps, stone placements with ember crackle, shuffle steps, a held-breath duck, then the red brick slamming into the wall and dropping back. No music.
