## Facts used (keep identical everywhere)
| On screen | Fact |
|---|---|
| 109x wider than Earth | Sun diameter 1,392,700 km / Earth 12,742 km = 109. About 1.3 million Earths fit inside the Sun by volume. |
| 6,500x wider than the Sun | Solar System out to Neptune's orbit, about 9 billion km across (about 60 AU) / Sun diameter = ~6,500. |
| 100 million x wider than the Solar System | Milky Way is about 100,000 light-years across (estimates run higher, so always say "about"). |
| 930,000x wider than the Milky Way | Observable universe is about 93 billion light-years across. |
- Always say "to Neptune's orbit" (the Solar System has no single edge) and "observable universe", never "the universe".
- Label it a thought experiment, not to scale (sizes in the visuals are compressed). Visuals are AI-assisted and 3D-rendered.
