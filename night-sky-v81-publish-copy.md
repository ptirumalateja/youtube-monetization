| Andromeda, 2.5 million years | About 2.5 million light-years |
| "From there, you'd see Earth as it was 2.5 million years ago. No modern humans yet." | Light that leaves Earth now takes about 2.5 million years to reach Andromeda, so an observer there sees Earth as it was then. Modern humans (Homo sapiens) are about 300,000 years old. |
- Say "the light you see left X ago", never "the star is gone" or "dead" (most still exist).
- The counter shows the age of the light, not the flight time. It climbs from 1.3 s to 2.5 million years and only goes up.
- Labelled "thought experiment / not to scale" (the pull-back view puts the two galaxies close together). Visuals are AI-assisted and 3D-rendered.
