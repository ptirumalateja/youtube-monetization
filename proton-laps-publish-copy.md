## Facts checked
- Light: 299,792.458 km/s; Earth's equator 40,075 km, so 7.48 laps per second.
- LHC Run 3 beam energy 6.8 TeV per proton: gamma about 7,247, speed 99.99999905% of c, so about 2.85 m/s slower than light (rounded on screen as 99.999999%).
- LHC ring 26.659 km; protons lap it about 11,245 times per second.
