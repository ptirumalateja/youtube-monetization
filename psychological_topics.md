
Tier 1: The Engagement Monsters (Highest Comments & Saves)

These concepts force the user to actively participate, leave a comment, or save the video to show someone else.
• #8. Your Brain Erases What You See (Troxler Fading)
	• Algorithm Trigger: Ultra-High Retention & Completion Rate.
	• Why it works: Instagram heavily rewards videos that users watch until the very last second. Because this is a literal biological experiment, viewers must stare at the dot for 15–20 seconds to see if it works.
	• Engagement Play: The comment section will explode with people typing "It didn't work for me" or "Wow, the blue completely vanished!"
• #1. The Ball That Never Falls (Your Top Pick)
	• Algorithm Trigger: High Rewatch Rate & Shareability.
	• Why it works: Anticipation anxiety is a powerful psychological hook. Denying the payoff right at the edge makes the viewer tense. When the camera spins and reveals the hole was an illusion, it creates a "micro-dopamine hit" that makes people want to share it to see if their friends fall for it too.

🔄 Tier 2: The Loop Kings (Highest Watch Time & Reach)

These concepts rely on seamless transitions to trick the algorithm into thinking a user is watching the video 2 or 3 times consecutively.
• #3. The Countdown That Won't End
	• Algorithm Trigger: Infinite Loop Metric.
	• Why it works: Because the countdown suddenly snaps back to 10 right as it hits 00:01, a viewer won't realize the video has restarted for at least 3 to 4 seconds. Instagram registers this as 130%+ watch time, which pushes the Reel into the global Explore and Reels feeds immediately.
• #4. The Staircase With No Bottom
	• Algorithm Trigger: Hypnotic Retention.
	• Why it works: The continuous, rhythmic motion of traveling down a staircase induces a mild trance state. If the loop is completely seamless—where the last frame perfectly matches the first frame—the viewer will sit through a couple of cycles before realizing they are trapped.

🎭 Tier 3: The Debate Starters (Highest Comment Threads)

These concepts trigger the human urge to argue, analyze, or guess what is happening in the comment section.
• #5. One Millimetre From Disaster
	• Algorithm Trigger: Comment Volatility.
	• Why it works: The hook promises a crash. The blade stops. The glass breaks for an unexpected reason (e.g., a sudden sound wave, a drop of water, or it just shatters from internal tension). Viewers will flood the comments arguing about the physics of why the glass actually broke.
• #6. Something Is Wrong With This Hallway
	• Algorithm Trigger: Rewatch Analysis.
	• Why it works: The hook asks, "Did you notice the door?" This forces the viewer to scrub backward or rewatch the video to spot the exact moment the geometry shifted. Every time they rewind to analyze the background, your metrics skyrocket.
