## Scientific facts / final-frame QA

- Five major layers, from Earth's surface upward: **troposphere**, **stratosphere**, **mesosphere**, **thermosphere**, **exosphere**.
- Approximate educational boundaries: troposphere **0–12 km** (varies by latitude/season); stratosphere **~12–50 km**; mesosphere **~50–85 km**; thermosphere **~85–600/700 km** (varies by convention); exosphere extends roughly to **10,000 km** and fades gradually into space. Boundaries are not solid walls.
- Most weather occurs in the troposphere; ozone-rich stratospheric air absorbs much incoming solar UV; many visible meteors ablate in the mesosphere; auroras occur in the upper atmosphere, and the ISS orbits roughly **400 km** up in the thermosphere. The ionosphere overlaps multiple named layers; it is not one of the five independent major layers.
- If a jet appears above the clouds, avoid suggesting commercial passenger aircraft routinely fly at ~50 km. Aircraft belong in the lower atmosphere. Avoid a solid ozone shield or UV rays bouncing like lasers.
- Screen artwork is **illustrative and not to scale**; the exosphere is not a hard boundary.
- Reference: NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-atmosphere/earths-atmosphere-a-multi-layered-cake/ and https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/earths-atmospheric-layers-3/
