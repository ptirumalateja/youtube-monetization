## Description
The flashes weren't inside the storm. They were above it. ⚡

For decades, pilots reported red flashes high above thunderstorms. Few believed them, until a camera test at the University of Minnesota in July 1989 caught one by accident. Today they are called red sprites, and astronauts on the International Space Station have photographed them from orbit.

Sprites are brief electrical flashes above thunderstorms, about 80 km (50 miles) up according to NASA, and they last only milliseconds.
